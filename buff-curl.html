The daily dilemma

The impossible choice we
refused to make.

For curly athletes, every day is a negotiation. Work out and your scalp suffers. Wash and your curls suffer. Skip both and reach for dry shampoo — white cast, crunchy curls, clogged follicles.

Work outScalp gets sweaty and itchy.
Wash itCurls lose their natural oils and moisture.
Dry shampooWhite dust, crunch, buildup.
Do nothingCompromise on both fronts.

The aha moment

“What if that mousse also cleaned my scalp?”

A $5 drugstore mousse gave me the softest Day-2 curls I'd ever had. It just didn't do anything for sweat or scalp health. That gap was the whole product — micellar foam that lifts sweat and oil while keeping curls soft. One step. No white cast. No stiffness.

Why you'll love it

Your friend in the in-between days.
