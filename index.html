Plate I

Research

Fractal Intelligence

Position paper · sole-authored · NordiCHI ’26, ACM Adjunct Proceedings · accepted August 2026

A metaphor borrows an image.An architecture inherits a mechanism.

AI design usually places AI at the centre of the human–nature–technology relationship. This paper inverts the diagram — decentering AI, treating it as one factor among several orbiting the human — and from that vantage develops two original concepts.

Fractal intelligence is the claim that AI does not merely imitate nature but structurally inherits it: intelligence whose architecture reproduces the recursive organizational principles observable from dendrite to network to ecosystem. McCulloch and Pitts formalized the neuron’s threshold logic in 1943, and every layered network since carries that inheritance.

The pooling effect accounts for how simultaneous concentrations of attention and capital render a technology culturally load-bearing, misusable, and capable of overriding commons-based rights — read against the Nordic tradition of allemansrätt.

Structural, not poeticBiomimicry is the practice; fractal intelligence is the property that makes it possible.
Fractal policyGovernance that repeats its integrity from constitutional principle to individual case.

Plate II

Systems

Belief Debate Engine

Independent product design & full-stack build · Next.js, React, GPT-4o, Exa Neural Search · 2026

Two agents. Opposing sides.One belief graph.

An AI research platform that deploys two adversarial agents to independently investigate opposing sides of a contested question — then studies how people evaluate conflicting information at the interaction model.

The interface surfaces calibrated uncertainty rather than a single answer: a real-time belief graph of confidence-weighted nodes, streamed over SSE as the research runs.

Adversarial by designTwo agents, no shared context, no house view.
Calibrated, not confidentNode weight is confidence. Disagreement stays visible.
Six research phasesCoordinated passes per debate, streamed live.
50–80 live sourcesSynthesized per question, cited at the node.

I start every project with a question: what does this actually need to communicate, and to whom?

I'm Shambhavi — a designer, builder and researcher with a Media Arts and Sciences (Design) degree from The GAME School at ASU, and a habit of starting with "why" before touching a typeface.

My work spans brand identity, packaging, digital product design, editorial systems, instructional design and organizational strategy. For a year at the AI Cloud Innovation Center at ASU I translated complex AI and AWS architectures into visual narratives legible to public-sector audiences without an engineering background — and facilitated the sessions where technical and non-technical stakeholders had to reach shared goals.

Since July 2026 I've run VisiWise Optimization LLC, giving small businesses the technical capacity they rarely hold in-house. Alongside it I build AI-native tools — the Belief Debate Engine uses multi-agent architecture and structured belief graphs to research both sides of a question with calibrated uncertainty.

My research on fractal intelligence — the argument that AI's architecture inherits nature's recursive structure rather than merely imitating it — was accepted to NordiCHI ’26 and appears in the ACM Adjunct Proceedings this October. I mentor at the Principled Innovation Academy hackathon at ASU; the teams I worked with went on to win.

Outside client work I build from curiosity: Buff Curl began as a personal product gap, (un)structure as a philosophical framework, and Stapled as a question about why finding zines is so much harder than finding books.
