Multi-Agent Epistemic Research System

Truth hastwo sides.

Two AI agents independently research opposing sides of any question using live web sources, structured belief graphs, and semantic contradiction detection. Not another chatbot — a belief accumulation engine.

0
research phases
0

2
agents, zero shared context

The interface surfaces calibrated uncertainty instead of an answer. Node weight is confidence; edges are the claims each agent used to get there; the whole thing streams over SSE as the research runs.

Where both agents reach a claim neither can settle, it stays marked contested — you see the disagreement rather than an average of it.

Designed and built end to end. Next.js, React, GPT-4o, Exa Neural Search.

The Research

Two original concepts

A sole-authored position paper, peer-reviewed and accepted to NordiCHI ’26, proposing two original frameworks for decentering AI in human–nature–technology design.

AI does not merely imitate nature — it structurally inherits it. Intelligence whose architecture reproduces the recursive organizational principles found from dendrite to network to ecosystem. A metaphor borrows an image; an architecture inherits a mechanism.

When attention and capital concentrate on one technology at once, it becomes culturally load-bearing — and load-bearing things are misusable. At sufficient scale they can override commons-based rights that were never up for negotiation.

Accepted August 2026 · NordiCHI ’26, the 14th Nordic Conference on Human-Computer Interaction · ACM Adjunct Proceedings, forthcoming October 2026. The paper is not yet published — ask Silva for a summary, or get in touch.
